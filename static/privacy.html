YT Lead Gen

Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 26, 2026

What We Collect

YT Lead Gen collects no personal data. We do not require accounts, logins, emails, or any form of registration. The only data we receive is the YouTube channel URLs or @handles you submit through the form.

How We Process Data

When you submit YouTube channel URLs, our server visits the public YouTube pages for those channels, captures screenshots of their Videos tab, and compiles them into a PDF document. This processing happens on our servers and is fully automated.

Data Retention

  • Generated PDF files are automatically deleted after 10 minutes
  • Screenshots taken during processing are deleted immediately after the PDF is created
  • We do not store, archive, or back up any generated reports
  • No submitted URLs are logged or saved permanently

Cookies & Tracking

We do not use cookies, analytics, tracking pixels, or any third-party tracking services. There is no advertising on this site.

Third-Party Services

  • YouTube — We access publicly available YouTube channel pages. We are not affiliated with YouTube or Google.
  • Google Fonts — We load the Inter font from Google Fonts for styling.
  • Tailwind CSS CDN — We load Tailwind CSS from their CDN for styling.

Rate Limiting

To prevent abuse, we limit requests to 3 per IP address per hour. We use your IP address solely for rate limiting purposes and do not store it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, open an issue on our GitHub repository.
